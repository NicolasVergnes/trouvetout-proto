Where did I put it?

Say in two seconds where you put something. Months later, ask for it in your own words: the answer is a place. No account, no server: everything stays on your phone.

Put away

“Passport in the top drawer of the dresser.” The object and the place are recognised, the place created if it is new. From the widget, the Android tile or the iPhone Action button.

Find

“The Kindle charger” finds the “white USB-C cable”. Typos, synonyms and approximations are fine; the answer fits on one line: place, container, date, photo.

Keep track

“I'm taking the drill”, “I lent the tent to Mark”, “remind me about the passport the day before 12 March”. Boxes move with their contents.

Your data stays yours

  • No data collected, no account, no advertising: privacy policy.
  • Search and its artificial-intelligence model run on the phone, even in airplane mode.
  • Encrypted database, optional lock, sensitive objects hidden from widgets and notifications.
  • Full export in one file readable without the app (data format), optional encrypted backup to a file of your choice, total wipe in one action.

Price

Six months of full trial, no account. After that, only new additions need the purchase — €10 once, or €2 a year; everything you recorded stays viewable, editable and exportable.

Phones

iPhone with iOS 16 or later; Android 10 or later. Photo search and names suggested from a photo need a phone with 6 GB of memory or more; everything else works on the others.
