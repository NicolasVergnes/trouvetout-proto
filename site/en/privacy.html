Privacy policy

Updated 8 November 2026. Publisher: glopglopdev — glopglopdev@gmail.com.

The app collects no data. Your objects, places and photos stay on your phone. There is no account, no server, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking of any kind.

We have no way to see what you record: the app sends us nothing, and what you enter stays under your sole control, on your device. If you write to us, we receive your address and your message, used only to answer you. Apple and Google give us sales statistics and, if you agreed to it on your phone, crash reports; none of your objects is in them. Search, including its artificial-intelligence model, runs entirely on your phone; the model ships with the app and downloads nothing. On Android, the app has no network access of its own.

The only exchanges that go through your phone

Besides what you export or print yourself, two exchanges go through your phone:

  1. The app store. To know whether you bought the app, it asks the store (App Store or Google Play) when it opens, even if you bought nothing. This exchange and purchases are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms; none of your objects is part of it.
  2. The encrypted backup, if you turn it on. It is written to a file you choose, for instance in your iCloud Drive or Google Drive. The service hosting the file receives an unreadable file, of which it sees the size and dates.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your objects, places and sentences

Everything you enter is stored in an encrypted database (SQLCipher), in the app's private storage, excluded from Apple's and Google's automatic backups. Every action is kept in a dated history, used to undo and to find where an object was. The sentence you said or typed is kept as is in that history and in your exports, including the sentence of a reminder.

Microphone and dictation

Speech recognition happens on the phone only: the app refuses any recognition that would go through a server. On Android 10 and 11 phones, where that guarantee does not exist, you type instead. Sound is never recorded; only the recognised sentence is kept, like a typed one. The microphone is asked for at first use, after an explanation.

The microphone key of your keyboard belongs to the keyboard, not to the app: depending on the keyboard, it may go through a server. On Android, a setting stops the keyboard from learning what you type in the app; iPhone offers no such setting.

Photos and the search model

Photos you attach are re-encoded: their metadata (GPS position, camera, capture date) are removed. They stay in the app's private storage, and the app never puts them in your gallery; on Android, pictures are taken through the phone's Camera app, some of which keep their own copy. Photos are not encrypted by the app: they are protected by its private storage and by your phone's encryption. The photo of a photo search and the photo of a pending capture are never kept (on Android, the temporary file handed back by the Camera app is deleted as soon as it is read). For search, the app computes numerical fingerprints of your entries and photos on the phone; they are a cache, never exported, and erased by the total wipe. The app also counts, on the phone, the entries opened from search to rank them better; this counter is neither exported nor sent.

Lock and sensitive objects

The optional lock uses your phone's own authentication (fingerprint, face or passcode); the app has no password of its own and never sees your biometric data. An object marked sensitive appears in no widget, notification, suggestion or thumbnail.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone; nothing goes through a server. A notification names the object at most, never its place; for a sensitive object it stays generic. On the lock screen your phone decides: the name stays visible if it is set to show everything. Your phone may keep that text (notification history, connected watch, an app allowed to read notifications); the total wipe does not erase those copies. On Android, after a restart or an update, the app opens briefly in the background to put your reminders back in place; nothing is sent.

Widgets, tile, Siri and Shortcuts

The widget, the quick-settings tile, the Action button and Shortcuts open the capture; they show no object.

Free trial

The app keeps the start date of your free trial on the phone, even after the total wipe of your data, and on iPhone even after it is uninstalled (in the keychain). It is only a date, sent to no one.

Encrypted backup

The backup contains all your data, sensitive objects included. It is encrypted before being written, with a recovery key that you write down: without it nobody can open it, not even us. The app keeps a copy of that key in its encrypted database to write the next backups; the total wipe erases it. The total wipe does not erase the backup file itself.

Export and printing

Export creates a ZIP file readable without the app (data format), which you share wherever you like; it is not encrypted and holds everything, sensitive objects included. The app keeps a copy for one day at most in its private storage, then deletes it (so does the total wipe). If you print a place or a container, the document goes to the printer or printing service you choose, handled by your phone. Once printed or saved as PDF, it is outside the app and the total wipe does not reach it. Sensitive objects appear in it only if you revealed them.

Your rights

Your data is in your hands: export gives you a complete, readable copy (access, portability); the total wipe, in the settings, erases in one action the database, its key, photos, caches, preferences and scheduled reminders (erasure). What remains is the trial start date and the backup file you chose, unreadable without your key; the confirmation text says so. Whatever already left the app (exports, printed documents, the phone's notification history) is yours to delete if you wish.

This site

This site uses no cookie, script or tracker; its host keeps technical logs (IP address, page requested) under its own terms.
